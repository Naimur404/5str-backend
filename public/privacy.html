5Str — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-23 · Operator: 5Str · Contact: verify@5str.xyz

5Str is a local-business and attractions discovery platform for Bangladesh. This page explains what we collect, why, how it is stored, and how you can erase it. We do not sell user data to third parties. We do not share user data with advertisers.

What we collect

  • Account data — name, email, password hash. Used only for sign-in and to label content you create (reviews, submissions, favorites).
  • Approximate location — when you search for businesses near you. We round latitude/longitude to ~1km precision before storing it.
  • App activity — which businesses you view, which categories you browse, which offers you tap. Stored against an anonymous device ID (a random UUID generated by the app on first launch). When you sign in, the activity is also linked to your account.
  • Crash and performance logs — collected via Sentry for diagnostic purposes only.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your phone number, IMEI, advertising ID, MAC address, or any persistent device identifier other than the random anonymous UUID.
  • We do not collect contacts, SMS, photos, microphone, or call logs.
  • We do not run background tracking when the app is closed.

Why we collect activity data

  • To recommend businesses, attractions, and offers similar to ones you liked.
  • To rank trending businesses in your area.
  • To detect abuse (fraudulent reviews, spam submissions).
  • To fix bugs and improve the app over time.

Sharing

We do not share your personal data with advertisers, brokers, or other apps. The only sub-processors involved are infrastructure providers we operate (managed database, Cloudflare R2 for image storage, Meilisearch for search, Sentry for crash logs). Each is bound by their standard data-processing agreements.

Retention

  • Activity events: 90 days, then aggregated and deleted.
  • Account data: kept while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Crash logs: 30 days.

Your rights

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold for your account by emailing the address above.
  • Erasure — clear all of your activity data at any time from Settings → Data & Privacy → Clear my activity. The button calls our public endpoint POST /api/v1/me/data/clear which removes every event and signal row tied to your anonymous ID and your account.
  • Account deletion — email the address above and we will delete the account within 30 days.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Database backups are encrypted at rest.

Children

5Str is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

Material changes are announced in-app and on this page. The “Last updated” date at the top of the page tells you when we last changed it.
